## Live Routes & Structure (Mirrors `button-bear-ai-lab` & `MCX-AI-Labs/data-financial-analysis`)

- `/`: Interactive 5-tab CX Discovery & Persona Dashboard (`Decision`, `Personas & scenarios`, `Usability & 40-buyer panel`, `CRO split test & ideation`, `Brief & verification`).
- `/script/`: Interactive Teleprompter Recording Script with reading controls (`A−`, `A+`, `Hide screen cues`) and visual cue boxes.
- `/downloads/`: Downloadable research packet, reusable `ux-persona-builder` skill, prompts, and verification briefs.
- `/prompts/`: All 11 step-by-step prompts (Part 1: Find Your Personas & Part 2: Use AI as a Co-Pilot) ready to run or inspect.
