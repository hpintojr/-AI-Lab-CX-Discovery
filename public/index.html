Step 2 · Reusable AI Skill

ux-persona-builder workflow

Configured once to make persona discovery repeatable: 1) Detect multi-source inputs (product context, MIT/RAND studies, Reddit threads, G2 reviews), 2) Extract atomic observations tagged by source ID, 3) Cluster by Goal + Behavior (never demographics alone), 4) Synthesize persona cards, and 5) Run the Evidence Linter to cut or relabel unsupported claims.

Speed-to-Lead Sam · Inbound Sales Floor Director

Segment: Mortgage, Lending, Debt Settlement & Legal Intake (High Urgency)

Main Goal: Answer every inbound borrower/client call within 60 seconds—especially after 5 PM—and warm-transfer qualified leads to closers.

Biggest Pain Point: Losing $4,000+ commissions when evening calls hit voicemail, while refusing to spend weeks configuring Twilio SIDs, A2P 10DLC forms, or robotic voice trees.

“I don't want to learn how to become a software engineer just to get missed calls answered at 7 PM. Let me call a live demo number on my cell phone first—if it sounds human and books the appointment, I'll pay today.”
Obvious Persona 2 (Converge) · Grounded [Sources 2, 3]

Field-Crew Carlos · Local Home Services Owner

Segment: Multi-Crew HVAC, Solar, Roofing & Commercial Cleaning

Main Goal: Capture emergency service calls and Facebook lead forms automatically while out on job sites.

Biggest Pain Point: "Setup Fatigue." Signs up for GoHighLevel or HubSpot, sees an empty dashboard with 50 menus, gets overwhelmed in 15 minutes, and goes back to pen and paper.

“Every CRM promises automation, then drops you into an empty screen expecting you to write prompts and build pipelines from scratch. I run crews all day—I need someone to diagnose my lead flow and hand me a working system.”
Non-Obvious Persona 3 (Diverge) · Grounded [Source 4]

Agency Architect Alex · B2B Systems Consultant

Segment: Niche B2B Consultants & Vertical Micro-Agencies (Highest LTV Multiplier)

Why Easy to Overlook: Smaller share of raw website signups, but each buyer deploys 5 to 20 client sub-accounts and stays the longest.

Main Goal & Pain: Wants 1-click industry templates ("Snapshots"), clean webhook payload logs, and transparent token/minute burn dashboards. Hates mandatory sales calls.

“As a B2B systems consultant, I bring 10+ client accounts with me if the platform lets me clone proven industry workflows in one click and track client ROI cleanly.”
Traffic Trap (Exclude from Ads) · Grounded [Source 5]

Freebie-Hunter Sid · Solo Hobbyist [Non-ICP]

Segment: Unqualified Blog & "Free AI Tool" Search Traffic

Behavioral Signal: Consumes content around "how to build free AI voice agents," has zero inbound call volume, zero marketing budget, and <1% conversion.

Strategic Action: Just like the class case study ("Dabbler Dana" vs. "Corporate Carlos"), we exclude "free trial / DIY bot" keywords and gate our funnel around established business lead flow.

Step 8A · Survey Self-Critique (Removing AI Bias)

Following the class methodology, we forced the AI to audit its first draft of exit survey questions for leading bias:

  • Draft 1 (Biased / Leading): "How impressive and helpful was the Mercury CRM setup experience?" → Rejected (Assumes positive experience).
  • Revised (Neutral): "How easy or difficult was it to get your first test phone call working?"
  • Draft 2 (Vague): "Would you buy Mercury CRM?" → Rejected (No price or workflow anchor).
  • Revised (Grounded): "At what exact step did you stop or hesitate during setup, and what were you expecting to see instead?"
